The civilization of the twentieth century cannot be universal

except by being a dynamic synthesis of all the cultural values of all

civilizations. It will be monstrous unless it is seasoned with the

salt of negritude. For it will be the saviour of humanity.

Lopold Sdar Senghor, first president of Senegal. From an

address to the Ghanaian Parliament, February 1961

National Day, Senegal
