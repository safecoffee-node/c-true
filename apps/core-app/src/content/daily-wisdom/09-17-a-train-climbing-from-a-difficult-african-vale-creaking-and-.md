A train

climbing from a difficult African vale

creaking and creaking slow and absurd

It shrills and shrills...

Many lives

have drenched the land

where the rails lie

crushed under the weight of the engine

and the din of the third class...

Slow absurd and cruel

the African train...

Agostinho Neto, African Train

National Heroes Day (birthday of Agostinho Neto, first president), Angola

Ganesh Chaturthi (Hindu festival), Mauritius
