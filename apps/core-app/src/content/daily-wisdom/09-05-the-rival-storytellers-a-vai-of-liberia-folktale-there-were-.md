THE RIVAL STORYTELLERS

There were two famous storytellers who lived in the same town.

Each had heard of the other. One evening they met at a dinner party. When dinner was finished the first storyteller began talking. He told, among other things, of his travels to a neighboring country where everything was exceptionally big.

He said, I personally witnessed the passing of a bird so huge that it took seven days for its neck to go by.

The other storyteller sat up in his chair and said, You know, I believe you are right, because when I was there, I saw a tree so enormous that a team of ten strong men using axes were unable to cut it down, even after trying for six months.

That is not possible, said the first storyteller. How could it be that ten strong men couldnt cut a tree down in six months?

Well, if this tree didnt exist, asked the second storyteller, where would your bird have been able to sit down?
