When he woke up the sun was already three hours old, blazing on the township scene. He whipped open the thin curtains and looked outside. A group of young boys were playing football in the street. An ice-cream man and a firewood vendor were jangling their bells from the opposite ends of the road. Young girls sauntered from the grocers with loaves of bread or packets of milk, neat little green packages of meat and bottles of paraffin, frugal tots of cooking oil and bunches of chaoumoellier.

A mother with a blanket round her waist was slapping a naked child playing with a garden tap. The child was screaming wildly and stamping her feet.

Thorns, 1989
