I see people looking like fire coming from where the sun sets.

They have an iron rod. I dont know what sort of people they are.

When you look at him, even [his] hair is like fire. And if he tries to

shake his rod, all the people fall down. And that person, his war

will not be resisted for he will be too strong. And he will come

from where the sun sets.

When a highway is made through Kanyuambora, it will be used by

an enemy whom none will understand in speech. An iron-

mouthed animal will come and follow the highway.

Embu of Kenya elders quoting Mbogo wa Kirangi, a prophet

who predicted the coming of the white man
