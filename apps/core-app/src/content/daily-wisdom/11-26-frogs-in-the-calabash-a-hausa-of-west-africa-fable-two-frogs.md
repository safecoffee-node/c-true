FROGS IN THE CALABASH

Two frogs fell into a calabash filled wth milk and were unable to get out. The frogs were treading milk when one said to the other,

I am getting tired. Today is my last day on Earth. He gave up, sank, and died. The other was also tired, but kept on treading.

After some time, his movements turned the oil in the milk into butter and he climbed atop the pat and jumped out.

God has said, Stand up that I may help you!
