Dawn breaks when you come up over the horizon, for by day you are the Sun and dispel the darkness. You cast your rays and the

Two Kingdoms bask in festive brilliance. People wake up and start to their feet; it is you who roused them. They wash themselves, pick up their clothes and raise their arms in praise of your appearance.

Eulogy to the Sun by Akhenaten, ancient Egyptian king
