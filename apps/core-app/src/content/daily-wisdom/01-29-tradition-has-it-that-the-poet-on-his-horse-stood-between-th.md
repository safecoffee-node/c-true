Tradition has it that the poet on his horse stood between the massed opposing forces and, with a voice charged with drama and emotion, chanted the better part of the day until the men, smitten with force of his delivery, dropped their arms and embraced one another.

Said Sheikh Samatar, Somali scholar. Samatar describes the traditional power of the poet in Somalia
