Ti gwe Uthaaraga;

Oh, you who lights;

Njuu unthaarire;

Come and light for me;

Na uthaarire antu bakwa;

And light for my people;

Na into biakwa

And for my property;

Na uthaarire antu bangi;

And light for other people;

Baria ukenda.

Those you may like.
