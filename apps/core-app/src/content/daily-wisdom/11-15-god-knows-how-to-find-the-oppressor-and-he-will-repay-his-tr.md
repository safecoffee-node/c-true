God knows how to find the oppressor,

And he will repay his transgressions with blood.

Trust not that time will forget you,

For judgment regards a lifetime like an hour.

A mans reputation remains after death,

When his deeds will be heaped up around him.

Judgment is eternal;

Only a fool ignores it!

Advice from the Instruction of King Merikare, an ancient

Egyptian text written during the Eighteenth Dynasty (1570-c.

1342 B.C.)
