We are descended from india-rubber, and our great ancestor, if you

like, was Michelin, or rather the fellow you see on the posters

advertising that brand of tyres. Thats why our forefathers and the

slaves who were sent to America were able to adapt themselves to a

situation that was new to them.

Sylvain Bemba, Congolese writer. From The Dark Room, 1964
