Through wisdom I have dived

down into the great sea,

and have seized in the place of her depths

a pearl whereby I am rich.

I went down like the great iron anchor,

whereby men anchor ships for the night

on the high seas,

and I received a lamp which lighteth me,

and I came up by the ropes

of the boat of understanding.

Queen of Sheba, Ethiopian queen, Kebra Negast (Glory of

Kings), a fourteenth-century chronicle of the Solomonic

dynasty
