Through wisdom I have dived  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;down into the great sea,  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;and have seized in the place of her depths  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;a pearl whereby I am rich.  
I went down like the great iron anchor,  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;whereby men anchor ships for the night  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;on the high seas,  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;and I received a lamp which lighteth me,  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;and I came up by the ropes  
&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;of the boat of understanding.
