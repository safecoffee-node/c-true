The West is on the point of being able to do without man in the production of work....At the same time that work gets along without human life, at that same time it ceases to make human life its final aim; it ceases to value man. Man has never been so unhappy as at this moment when he is accumulating so much.

Cheikh Hamidou Kane, Senegalese writer. From Ambiguous
