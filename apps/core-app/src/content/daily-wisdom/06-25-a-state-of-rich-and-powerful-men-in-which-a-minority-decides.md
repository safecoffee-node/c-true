A State of rich and powerful men in which a minority decides and imposes its will, whether we agree or not, and whether we understand or not, would be the continuation in a new form of the situation against which we are struggling. The question of peoples power is the essential question of our revolution.

Samora Moises Machel, first president of Mozambique
