Ukiona mwasho juu ya kiganja cha mkono wa kulia utapokea fedha.

Kiganja cha mkono wa kushoto kikikuwasha ni alama ya kutumia fedha.

If you feel an itching on your right palm it means that you will receive money. If you feel an itching on your left palm it means you will spend money.
