Do what is right all the days of your life;  
Comfort the one who weeps;  
Do nothing to oppress the widow;  
Rob no man of the inheritance of his father;  
Respect the authority of the magistrates.

<div class="attribution">
Advice from the “Instruction of King Merikare,” an ancient
Egyptian text written during the Eighteenth Dynasty (1570-c.
1342 B.C.)
