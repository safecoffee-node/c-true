Song of the Mozambican Women

Lifting her eyes to the certainty of victory,

Knowing that victory was built through sacrifice

Who is it?

She who lifts high the beacon of freedom,

Who cries to the whole world

That our struggle is the same

Its the emancipated Mozambican woman

Who brings her courage to the People.
