Tamu Ya Pendo Ni Nini?

What Is The Sweetness of Love?

Nawauliza suali

enyi mnaopendana

mnipe jibu la kweli

jawabu yenye maana:

pendo ni kitu thakili

nahisi raha halina.

Tamu ya pendo ni nini

nambieni waungwana

nimefikiri yakini

si usiku su mchana

nimeshindwa kubaini

tamu yake kuiona.

I ask you a question

you who love one another,

give me a true answer,

a meaningful solution:

Love is a heavy thing,

I feel there is no joy in it.

What is the sweetness of love?

Tell me, gentlefolk,

I have been thinking deeply,

not by night alone, not by day alone;

I am at a loss to understand

or to see its sweetness.

Napenda toka zamani

si kama meanza jana

tamu yake siioni

illa adha kubwa sana

na adhabu ya moyoni

kufanya si mtu tena.

Nambieni waungwana

wawili mnaopendana

kweli raha mimi sina

pendo lanikera sana.

Na kama tamu halina

katu sitopenda tena.

I have loved for a long time,

it is not yesterday that I began,

but I do not see the sweetness of it;

nothing except a great deal of vexation,

and torment of the heart,

I am no longer capable of it.

Tell me, gentle folk,

who love one another in pairs,

indeed I am without peace,

love upsets me a great deal.

And if there is not sweetness in it,

I will never love again.
