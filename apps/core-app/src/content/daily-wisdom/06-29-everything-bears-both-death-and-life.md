Everything bears both death and life. Rain brings down lightning

and life-giving water, earth brings forth harvest and entombs the

dead, the sun spreads light and drought; the years bring on old age

and famine, children and Independence.

Ahmadou Kourouma, Ivorian writer. From The Suns of

Independence, 1968

Independence Day, Seychelles

Senufo of Cote d'Ivoire
