I no longer have a borrowed soul. I no longer have borrowed

thoughts or ideas. I no longer speak in a borrowed language.

Mobutu Sese Seko, first president of Zaire. Quoted in the San

Francisco Examiner and Chronicle, June 25, 1972. Seko explains

why his country changed its name from the Democratic

Republic of the Congo to Zaire
