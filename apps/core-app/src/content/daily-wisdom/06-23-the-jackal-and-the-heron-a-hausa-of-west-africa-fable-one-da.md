THE JACKAL AND THE HERON

One day a jackal was eating a chicken when a bone got caught in his throat. The jackal cried out for someone to help dislodge the bone, and offered his rescuer a big reward. The grey heron came forward, stuck its head in the jackals mouth, and with its long bill extracted the bone. The jackal turned to go on its way. The heron said, Wait a minute. Wheres my reward? The jackal replied,

Your reward is this: you put your head in my mouth and got away safely.
