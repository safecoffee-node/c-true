I have no intention of being an indifferent on-looker if the distant

powers have the idea of dividing up Africa, for Ethiopia has been

for more than fourteen centuries an island of Christians in the

middle of a sea of pagans.

Menelik II, emperor of Ethiopia

Christmas, celebrated throughout Africa

Family Day, Angola
