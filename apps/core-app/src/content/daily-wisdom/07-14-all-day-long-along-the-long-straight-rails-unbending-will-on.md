All day long along the long straight rails

(Unbending will on the listless sands)

Across the dryness of Cayor and Baol where

the arms of the baobabs twist in anguish

All day long, all along the line

Through tiny stations, each exactly like the last,

chattering little black girls uncaged from school

All day long, roughly shaken on the benches of

the clanking, dust-covered wheezing antique train

I come seeking to forget Europe in the pastoral heart of

Sine.

Lopold Sdar Senghor, poet and first president of Senegal.

From All Day Long

Day of Association, Senegal
