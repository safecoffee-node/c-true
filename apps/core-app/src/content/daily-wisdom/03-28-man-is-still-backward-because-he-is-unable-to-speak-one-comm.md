Man is still backward because he is unable to speak one common

language. Until he attains this human aspiration, which seems

impossible, the expression of joy and sorrow, what is good and

bad, beauty and ugliness, comfort and misery, mortality and

eternity, love and hatred, the description of colors, sentiments,

tastes, and moodsall will be according to the language each

people speaks.

Colonel Moammar Gadhafi, The Green Book, 1975-1981
