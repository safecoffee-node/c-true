While the Westerner asserts that character formation is the chief

thing, he forgets that character is formed primarily through

relations with other people, and that there is really no other way in

which it can grow. Europeans assume that, given the right

knowledge and ideas, personal relations can be left largely to take

care of themselves, and this is perhaps the most fundamental

difference in outlook between Africans and Europeans.

Jomo Kenyatta, first president of Kenya. From Facing Mount

Kenya, 1938
