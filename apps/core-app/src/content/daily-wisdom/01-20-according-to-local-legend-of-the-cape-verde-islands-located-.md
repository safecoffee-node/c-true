According to local legend of the Cape Verde Islands, located 385

miles (620 kilometers) off the west coast of Africa, when God had

finished creating the heavens and the earth, he rubbed his hands

together in satisfaction. Some crumbs fell from his hands and

scattered unnoticed into the Atlantic Ocean, creating the Cape

Verde islands. A similar legend is told in the Indian Ocean islands

of Africa.
