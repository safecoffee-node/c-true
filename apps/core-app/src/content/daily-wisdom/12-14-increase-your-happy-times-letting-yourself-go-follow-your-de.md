Increase your happy times, letting yourself go;

Follow your desire and best advantage.

And do your thing while you are still on this earth,

According to the command of your heart.

Song of Antuf. Inscribed on the wall of an ancient Egyptian

tomb during the Eighteenth Dynasty, c. 1300 B.c.
