South Africas oldest city, Cape Town, has long been known as the

tavern of the oceans. And indeed the countrys position midway

between the Orient and the Occident made it an ideal meeting-

place for people and ideas from both hemispheres.

Modern-day South Africa has often been called the laboratory of

mankind...a place where all races on earth are represented and

live cheek by jowl.

Piet Miller, South African journalist. From Insight Guides South

Africa, 1992
