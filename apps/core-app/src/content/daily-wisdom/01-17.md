<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-20">
    <span class="text-base font-sans font-bold">Pendo Halijui Siri</span>
    <span class="text-base font-sans font-bold">Love Does Not Know Secrets</span>
</div>

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-20">
    <p>
        Pendo halijui siri
        likifichwa hufichuka.
    </p>
    <p>
        Love knows no secrets,
when it is hidden it will be
discovered.
    </p>
</div>

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-20">
      <p>
            Pendo halina hiyari
            mtu linapomshika
            kulla jambo atakiri
            jambo lisilofanyika.
            </p>
    <p>
    Love has no choice;
when it seizes a man,
he will confess everything,
everything that was not done.
    </p>
</div>

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-20">
      <p>
            Pendo halina huruma
            kizee hufedheheka
            </p>
       <p>
    Love has no pity,
even an old man may be put to
shame,
</p>
</div>

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-20">
        <p>
            pendo halirudi nyuma
            kwa kitu linachotaka.
            Mtu likimuandama
            majununi hugeuka.
            </p>
       <p>
 love does not return
to a thing it desires.
When it pursues a man,
he turns mad.

</p>
</div>

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-20">
        <p>
            Pendo humdhili mtu
mwili wake hudhofika
akidhilika mwenzetu
haifai kumcheka
Pendo mtu hadhubutu
kando-kando kuliweka. 
            </p>
       <p>
 Love humbles a man,
his body becomes emaciated;
when a friend of ours is humiliated
it is not fair to laugh at him.
A man does not have the stamina
to put love aside.

</p>
</div>

<div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-20">
<p>
Pendo katu haliridhi
kitu kiwe cha shirika.
Pendo unapoliudhi
mara moja kuyayuka.
Pendo huwa ni maradhi
mabaya yasotibika.
</p>

<p>
Love never agrees
to share with anything.
If you irritate love,
you melt away at once.
Love is a disease,
a malignant incurable disease. 
</p>
</div>
