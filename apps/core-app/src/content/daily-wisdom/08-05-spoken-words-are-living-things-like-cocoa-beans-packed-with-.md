Spoken words are living things like cocoa-beans packed with life.

And like cocoa-beans they grow and give life.... They will enter

some insides, remain there and grow like the corn blooming on the

alluvial soil at the river side.

Gabriel Okara, Nigerian Writer. From The Voice, 1964

Farmers Day, Zambia
