You know that if everyone in the world were equally sophisticated,

the world would chew itself up. We cant all have the same amount

of intelligence. ... There will always be a certain number of igno-

rant people. There are those with their eyes open and those with

their eyes closed. If we all had our eyes openif you knew

everything that I knewthings just wouldn't work. Its like a

scale; as one end goes up, the other end necessarily must go back

down. ...If everyone knew the same thing, then there would be no

market.

Abdurraham Madu, African art merchant, 1991. From

Christopher B. Steiners African Art in Transit, 1994
