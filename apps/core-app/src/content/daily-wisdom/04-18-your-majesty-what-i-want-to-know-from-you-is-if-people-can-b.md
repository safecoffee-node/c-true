Your Majesty, what I want to know from you is if people can be bought

at any price....

Your Majesty, what I want to know from you is: Why do your people

kill me?

Do you kill me for following my stolen cattle which are seen in the

possession of the Mashonas....

I have called all white men living at or near Bulawayo to hear my

words, showing clearly that I am not hiding anything from them

when writing to Your Majesty.

Chief Lobengula, last Matabele king. From a letter to Queen

Victoria

Independence Day, Zimbabwe
