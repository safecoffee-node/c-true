In Tanzania it was more than one hundred tribal units which lost their freedom; it was one nation that regained it.

Julius K. Nyerere, first president of Tanzania. From a speech delivered at the University of Toronto, Canada, October 2, 1969

Freedom to many means immediate betterments, as if by magic. ... Unless I can meet at least some of these aspirations, my support will wane and my head will roll just as surely as the tickbird follows the rhino.

Nyerere, quoted in Time magazine, April 9, 1964
