Africa my Africa  
Africa of proud warriors or ancestral savannahs  
Africa that my grandmother sings  
On the bank of her distant river  
I have never known you  
But my face is full of your blood  
Your beautiful black blood which water the wide fields

<div class="attribution">David Mandessi Diop, Senegalese poet. First stanza of
“Africa,” in Coup de Pilons, 1956 </div>
