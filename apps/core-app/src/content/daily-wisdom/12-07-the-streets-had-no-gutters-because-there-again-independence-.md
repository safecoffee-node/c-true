The streets had no gutters, because there again Independence had

played false, they never dug the gutters they promised and they

never will; water will swamp the streets as always, and colonized

or independent, the African will keep on wading through them

until such times as God unpeels the curse stuck fast in their black

backsides.

Ahmadou Kourouma, Ivorian writer. From The Suns of

Independence, 1968

Independence Day, Cote d'Ivoire
