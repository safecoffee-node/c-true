In the time of happiness:

<ul class="no-bullets">
<li>Ships ply the river;</li>
<li>They move north and south.</li>
<li>Men build temples and dig ponds;</li>
<li>They make plantations of trees for the gods.</li>
</ul>

In the time of happiness:

<ul class="no-bullets">
<li>The people make merry:</li>
<li>They drink in the gladness of their hearts.</li>
<li>For a man can put his bed in the shade,</li>
<li>And sleep safely behind his own gate.</li>
</ul>
