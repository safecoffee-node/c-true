Mwenye kwenda kulala na kiu usiku, roho yake itakuwa inatangatanga; paka akiigundua ataivamia na mtu atakufa papo hapo.

If a person goes to bed thirsty, his soul will wander about at night; if a cat sees it she will pounce on it and the person will die at once.
