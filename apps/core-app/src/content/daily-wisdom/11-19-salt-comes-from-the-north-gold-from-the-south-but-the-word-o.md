Salt comes from the north, gold from the south, but the word of

God and the treasures of wisdom are to be found only in

Timbuctoo.

Timbuctoo, 1934

For some people, when you say Timbuktu it is like the end of the world, but this is not true.

Iam from Timbuktu, and I can tell you we are right at the heart of the world.

Ali Farka Toure, Malian musician

Ring of the Dogon of Mali
