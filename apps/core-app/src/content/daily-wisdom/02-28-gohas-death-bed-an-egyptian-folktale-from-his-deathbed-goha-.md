GOHAS DEATH BED

An Egyptian Folktale

From his deathbed Goha called to his wife. He asked her to fix her

long black hair neatly, apply her makeup generously, dress in her

most elegant clothes, and come sit by his bedside. His distraught

wife replied, I cant think of such frivolous things while you are

dying, and never will I again after you are gone. Goha demanded,

Iam a dying man, do as I say. His wife complied. Sitting beside

her husband in her finest gown, she asked, Dont you want to

gaze at my beauty before dying? Goha replied, No. They say

Death chooses the best and I hoped death might choose you

instead of me.

Union Day, Egypt
