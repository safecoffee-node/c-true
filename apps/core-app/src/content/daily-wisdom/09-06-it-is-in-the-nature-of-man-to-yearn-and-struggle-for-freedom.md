It is in the nature of man to yearn and struggle for freedom. The

germ of freedom is in every individual, in anyone who is a human

being. In fact, the history of mankind is the history of man

struggling and striving for freedom.

Chief Albert Luthuli of South Africa, former president of the

African National Congress and recipient of the Nobel peace

prize

Somhlolo (Independence Day), Swaziland
