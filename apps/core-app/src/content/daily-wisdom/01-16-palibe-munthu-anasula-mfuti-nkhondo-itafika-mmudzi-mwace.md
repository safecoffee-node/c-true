Palibe munthu anasula mfuti nkhondo itafika mmudzi mwace.

A person does not begin to forge a gun when the war has already

arrived in the village.

Nyanja of Malawi proverb on preparedness

John Chilembowe Day (commemorates the freedom fighters struggle against

British colonial rule), Malawi
