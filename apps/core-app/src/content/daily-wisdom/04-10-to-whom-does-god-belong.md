To whom does God belong? Who has not the right to love Him or to scoff at Him? Think it over. ... the freedom to love God or hate Him is Gods ultimate gift, which no one takes from man.

Cheikh Hamidou Kane, Senegalese writer. From Ambiguous

Adventure, 1962
