Another path, which conveniently twists as if in conformity with the often unsteady feet that stumble along it, brings you to the

Happy Bar, the tin-roofed dwelling where Maria Ssentamus sumptuous curves shake in perpetual merriment as she serves warm beer in thick, greasy glasses to the thirsty locals. Maria must surely weigh over fourteen stones. Nevertheless, she is by far the most seductive woman in the village, and she has a number of children to prove it. It seems that a simple shake of her magnificent hips is enough to bowl a man over. Few can withstand the onslaught. Members of the Mothers Union spitefully whisper that this unfailing charm is all something to do with what Maria puts in the drinks.
