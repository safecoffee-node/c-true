Beyond the refusal of all exterior domination is the urge to
reconnect in a deep way with Africa’s cultural heritage, which has
been far too long misunderstood and rejected. Far from being a
superficial or folkloric attempt to bring back to life some of the
traditions or practices of our ancestors, it is a matter of constructing a new African society, whose identity is not confused from
outside.
