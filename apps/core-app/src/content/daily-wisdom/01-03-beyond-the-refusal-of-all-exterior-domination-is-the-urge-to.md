Beyond the refusal of all exterior domination is the urge to

reconnect in a deep way with Africas cultural heritage, which has

been far too long misunderstood and rejected. Far from being a

superficial or folkloric attempt to bring back to life some of the

traditions or practices of our ancestors, it is a matter of construct-

ing a new African society, whose identity is not confused from

outside.

Paul Zoungrana, religious leader from Upper Volta (now

Burkina Faso)

Revolution Day (anniversary of the 1966 coup), Burkina Faso
