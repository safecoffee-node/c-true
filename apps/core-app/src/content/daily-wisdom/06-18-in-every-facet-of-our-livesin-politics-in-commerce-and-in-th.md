In every facet of our livesin politics, in commerce and in the professionsrobbery is the base line. And its been so from time.

In the early days, our forebears sold their kinsmen into slavery for minor items such as beads, mirrors, alcohol and tobacco. These days, the tune is the same, only the articles have changed into cars, transistor radios and bank accounts. Nothing else has changed, and nothing will change in the foreseeable future. But thats the problem of those of you who will live beyond tomorrow.

Ken Saro-Wiwa, Nigerian writer. From Africa Kills Her Sun
