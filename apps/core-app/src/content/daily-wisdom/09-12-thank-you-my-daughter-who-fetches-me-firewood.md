Thank you my daughter

who fetches me firewood.

There were two people

who were in seclusion

who won the raid.

Nowadays

there are no raids.

Thank you my son

for winning the raid.

Thank you my daughter

who collects firewood for me.

Nowadays

there are no raids.

Education is the raid

The book is the shield

The pen is the spear

Raid is on ignorance

Raid is on ignorance.

Kalenjin of Kenya childrens song
