THE HARE AND THE LION

An Ethiopian Fable

by Lokman (c. 1100 B.c.)

A hare upon meeting a lioness one day said reproachfully, I have

always a great number of children, while you have only one or two

now and then. The lioness replied, That is true, but my one child

is a lion.
