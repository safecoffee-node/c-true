The British, the Ethiopians and the Italians

are squabbling,

The country is snatched and divided by

whoever is stronger,

The country is sold piece by piece

without our knowledge,

And for me, all this is the teeth of

the last days of the world.

Faarah Nuur, Somali poet and clan leader

Independence Day, Somalia

Independence Day, Madagascar
