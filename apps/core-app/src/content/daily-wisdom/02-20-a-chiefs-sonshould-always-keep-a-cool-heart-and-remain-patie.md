A chief's son...should always keep a cool heart and remain

patient. Rushing through things at a gallop, we risk burying

someone alive; and a hasty tongue can ensnare us in troubles we

cannot escape by flight.

Ahmadou Kourouma, Ivorian writer. From The Suns of

Independence, 1968
